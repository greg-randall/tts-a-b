About the Ranking System

Rating: The Elo rating represents the estimated skill level of each voice. The baseline is 1000.

Confidence Interval: Shows the uncertainty in the rating. Wider intervals (larger ± values) indicate less certainty.

Ranking Confidence: Indicates how trustworthy the rating is based on the number of matches played: High (80-100%), Medium (50-79%), Low (0-49%)
